### 1. Executive Summary & Context
Housing societies across Mira Road face growing frustration with proprietary gate apps: annual recurring software fees (₹25,000–₹60,000), intrusive resident advertisements, hardware breakdowns during monsoon power outages, and legal exposure under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** due to centralized biometric data harvesting. Furthermore, arbitrary 1-star ratings and gate disputes destabilize domestic staffing.

**Project Maanyata** proposes a **30-day, zero-risk ground pilot (November 5 – December 4, 2026)** at Maryland Palms CHS. Built 100% client-side with zero cloud debt, Maanyata modernizes gate clearance and worker recognition at **₹0 recurring cost**, **zero hardware procurement**, and **zero cloud surveillance**.

---

### 2. The Four Operational Pillars

1. **Zero Recurring Cost & Hardware Independence**  
   - **₹0 Forever:** No per-flat subscription, zero server fees, zero software lock-in.  
   - **No Gate Hardware:** Security guards use existing smartphones (`verify.html`). Non-smartphone workers carry durable ₹10 laminated CR80 physical pass cards.

2. **Zero Surveillance & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance**  
   - **No Central Honeypot:** Worker credentials live locally on their own device or physical card. The society stores no biometric records.  
   - **Anti-Burglary Unit Masking:** Flat numbers are automatically masked (`B-402` $\to$ `B-***`) in public links, preventing reconnaissance of vacant flats.  
   - **Cryptographic Fingerprint:** Deterministic 8-character ID (`#M-XXXX-XXXX`) prevents pass counterfeiting.

3. **100% Offline Reliability (Sub-300ms Verification)**  
   - **Zero Network Dependency:** Gate scans execute client-side via Ed25519 cryptographic signatures and localized pure-JS QR decoding.  
   - **Monsoon & Basement Proof:** Functions flawlessly in stilt parking and basement cell-dead zones during severe weather or power cuts.

4. **Worker Dignity & Reverse Attestation**  
   - **Zero Punitive Blacklists:** Replaces toxic 1-star reviews with verified tenure mathematics and positive skill attestations (Cooking, Cleaning, Eldercare).  
   - **Decoupled Key Custody:** Trust with house keys is certified as an earned behavioral trait without publishing flat numbers.  
   - **10 Dignity Charter Principles:** Establishes humane standards (water access, lift access, weather delay grace, prompt monthly settlements) that drastically reduce staff turnover.
