SUBJECT:
Proposal for a 30-Day Zero-Cost Sovereign Gate Verification & Domestic Staff Trust Pilot

1. Executive Summary & The Problem with Proprietary Gate Apps

Housing societies across Mira Road face mounting challenges with commercial gate SaaS platforms: recurring annual subscriptions (₹25,000–₹60,000 AMC), intrusive resident advertising, gate lockouts during monsoon cellular/power outages, and significant legal liability under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 due to centralized biometric data harvesting. Furthermore, arbitrary 1-star ratings and gate disputes destabilize domestic staffing across Maryland Palms. Project Maanyata proposes a 30-day, zero-risk ground pilot (Nov 5 – Dec 4, 2026) offering high-speed gate verification, zero cloud surveillance, and authentic worker dignity at ₹0 financial cost and zero hardware procurement.

2. Four Core Operational Pillars

Zero Recurring Cost & Hardware-Free

₹0 software license forever. Operates entirely on security guards' existing smartphones via verify.html. Non-smartphone staff carry durable ₹10 laminated CR80 physical cards.

Zero Surveillance & DPDP Compliance

No central biometric database or cloud honeypot. Automatic address masking (B-402 $\to$ B-***) prevents casing of vacant flats. Cryptographic 8-character ID prevents identity spoofing.

100% Offline Reliability (Sub-300ms)

Ed25519 cryptographic signatures and localized jsQR verify credentials entirely client-side. 100% operational in basement/stilt parking dead zones and severe monsoon blackouts.

Worker Dignity & Reverse Attestation

Eliminates toxic 1-star blacklists. Residents attest positive skills and tenure. Incorporates the 10 Dignity Charter principles (water, lunch break, lift access) to minimize staff turnover.
