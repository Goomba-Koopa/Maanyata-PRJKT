2. **One Dog Minimum:** Point escort, early warning radar, combat distraction.
3. **Multiple Torches (Non-Negotiable):** Complete spawn suppression; zero dark corners.
4. **Battle Gear:** Shield, sword, and full tactical readiness.
5. **Solid Building Blocks:** Mandatory crouch-bridging over drowned waters.
6. **Time & Water Fountain Exit:** Anti-greed timer; exfiltrate via the vertical water lift.
7. **Provisions:** Cooked fish in quantity; hunger halts regeneration.
8. **Canine Rations:** Rotten flesh reserved exclusively for dog triage.
* **The Golden Law of Retreat:** *Withdraw to the surface the instant health drops to half. Continuing past that point relies on luck; the purpose of an expedition is to return with resources.*
