Four Pillars of Sovereign Trust

Engineered specifically for the operational, legal, and human realities of cooperative housing societies in Maharashtra.

MCS Act 1960 Model Bye-Law 138/139 Alignment

Provides Managing Committees and General Bodies with sovereign record-keeping authority. Societies maintain verified staff registries with zero third-party custody, eliminating arbitrary private app blacklists.

Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act § 138-139

Zero Cloud Debt & Offline Cryptography

Passes are cryptographically signed using Ed25519 envelopes (MNY1.<body>.<sig>). Gate security checks decode QR payloads directly on the device substrate, guaranteeing 100% operation during cellular blackouts.

ISO/IEC 18004 • RFC 8032 Asymmetric Signatures

DPDP Act 2023 Sovereign Privacy Vault

Worker identity verification extracts only cryptographic SHA-256 digests and masked XXXX-XXXX-1234 identifiers. Raw 12-digit UID numbers and biometric files are instantly purged from memory, preventing corporate surveillance leaks.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

Universal Worker Dignity Charter

Every pass guarantees non-negotiable working conditions: access to clean drinking water, sanitary washrooms, extreme weather delay relief, and agreed rest days. Seniority milestones are permanently attested by resident signatures.
