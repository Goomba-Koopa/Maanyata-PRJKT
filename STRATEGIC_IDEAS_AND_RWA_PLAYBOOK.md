## 2. The Four Statutory Legal Shields

When presenting to Managing Committees, secretaries, or legal advisors, cite these exact statutes to eliminate administrative inertia:

### Pillar 1: Maharashtra Co-operative Societies (MCS) Act, 1960
* **Model Bye-Law No. 138 & 139 (Operational Powers & Petty Cash):**
  * *Operational Reality:* The Managing Committee (Secretary & Treasurer) has the direct authority to approve routine operational, administrative, safety, and stationery expenses out of petty cash without convening an Annual General Body Meeting (AGM) or Special General Body Meeting (SGBM).
  * *Rebuttal to "We need an AGM vote":* A pilot under ₹349/wing (for laminating passes and passbook token printing) requires zero general body delay. It can be signed on a Sunday morning.
* **Model Bye-Law No. 145 & 146 (Capital Works):**
  * Maanyata has ₹0 software cost, ₹0 cloud debt, and ₹0 licensing. It is an operational safety protocol, not a capital expenditure.

### Pillar 2: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
* **Section 4 & Section 6 (Consent & Purpose Limitation):** Personal data processing requires informed, verifiable consent.
* **Section 8 (Mandatory Security Safeguards):** The Data Fiduciary (the Housing Society) MUST implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches.
  * *The Trap:* An open paper register at the gate—where visitors, delivery boys, and strangers photograph the names, phone numbers, and addresses of female workers—is a direct, indefensible breach of Section 8.
* **Section 33 & First Schedule (Penalties):** Prescribes statutory civil penalties up to ₹250 Crores for failure to prevent personal data breaches.

### Pillar 3: Aadhaar Act, 2016
* **Section 29 (Prohibition on Publishing & Unmasked Storage):** Forbids displaying, publishing, or openly filing unredacted 12-digit Aadhaar numbers.
* **Section 38 & 40 (Penalties for Identity Theft/Leakage):** Imposes criminal liability for unauthorized exposure of identity data.
* *Maanyata Defense:* Raw Aadhaar is never stored. Identity is cryptographically masked client-side (`XXXX-XXXX-4921`) and signed via Ed25519. Zero identity data exists on the device.

### Pillar 4: Police Commissionerate Verification Orders
* **Statutory Basis:** Section 144 CrPC / Section 163 BNSS (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023) promulgated by MBVV Police Commissionerate.
* **The Society Liability:** Failure to maintain verified domestic worker records exposes committees to notices under Section 188 IPC / Section 223 BNS.
* *Maanyata Solution:* One-click verified PDF export of the entire society worker roster with police clearance dates and unit attestations.
