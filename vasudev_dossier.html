2.0 Statutory Justification: MCS Act 1960 Sec 81 & Model Bye-Laws

Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 — Section 81 & Bye-laws 160–168

Section 81 mandates periodic administrative and financial audits, holding office bearers strictly liable for vigilance failures, negligence in premises maintenance, and unauthorized service vendor disbursements. Model Bye-law 160 establishes the Committee’s affirmative duty to enforce security protocols for domestic staff without inflicting unlawful discrimination or privacy infringement.

DPDP Act 2023 Statutory Harmony: Conventional gate systems store unmasked Aadhaar scans on insecure private cloud servers, exposing the Managing Committee to severe regulatory penalties under Section 8 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
